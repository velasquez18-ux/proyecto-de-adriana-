My Story

My Experience as a Social Communicator

When I worked as a social communicator, I discovered that photography was the part that I was most passionate about. However, at that time, I did not have the opportunity to dedicate myself completely to it. Because my voice is strong, I had the opportunity to work for two years at a radio station. It was a very rewarding experience because I learned to control my voice, improve my pronunciation, and capture the attention of listeners without them being able to see me. Each program represented a new challenge and an opportunity to communicate with clarity, emotion, and confidence. Thanks to this experience, I developed skills that I value greatly today and that helped me grow both personally and professionally.

In addition to radio, I have participated in institutional communication projects, photography coverage, and social media content production. I am interested in telling stories that connect, educating through clear content, and creating messages that strengthen the identity of brands and projects.
